Why session windows, not fixed?

A package's journey has no fixed duration — it's bounded by inactivity, the same shape as a user session. EventTimeSessionWindows.with_gap(20min) accumulates a package's scans until that gap elapses, then hands the whole group to one function call — sort, then judge, rather than judging incrementally on partial data.

Why two separate jobs?

Stuck-package detection is the simplest real anomaly — one timer per key, reset on every scan. Journey correlation is the hard one — full-session out-of-order stitching. Building and proving the simple one first (Checkpoint 4) de-risked the event-time/watermark foundation before the harder join (Checkpoint 5) needed it.

What's verified live, not just unit-tested?

Both jobs run against the real Dockerized Flink cluster + Kafka, not just pytest. A clean 4-station journey produces no alert; a skipped station produces an accurate one; a duplicate scan after Dispatch (caught by review, fixed, retested) correctly produces no alert either.
