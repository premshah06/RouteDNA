Why key by package_id?

Station (4 values) would only ever fill 4 of 6 partitions and gives no per-package order. Random/event_id keying spreads load but scrambles a package's own scan order across partitions. package_id is the only key that gives the one guarantee the downstream join actually needs.

Why doesn't Kafka gate routing?

route_for() answers before publish() is awaited — publish runs as a tracked asyncio.Task. A slow or momentarily unreachable broker delays analytics, never the conveyor belt.

What's tested where?

Fast suite (9 tests) uses an in-memory fake producer, no Docker needed. One test is marked kafka and runs against the real broker — produce via gRPC, consume back, decode, assert.
